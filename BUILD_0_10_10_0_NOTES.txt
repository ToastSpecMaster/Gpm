GRAND PRIX MANAGER 0.10.10.0
HISTORICAL CAREER, REGULATION & CONTRACT OVERHAUL

BUILD STATUS
- 0.10.10.0 = complete development build, not yet released.
- Do not label this build 0.10.10.1 until it has been tested and approved as a release.

WHAT CHANGED

1. SEASON-CORRECT HISTORICAL GRIDS (2016–2026)
- Added a one-time save migration that rebuilds a historical career's grid from the selected season's constructor and driver entries.
- A 2019 career now starts from the 2019 season grid rather than retaining the 2016 grid.
- Existing constructor careers use the selected season's official starting line-up unless an explicitly player-negotiated, unexpired contract overrides a seat.
- Custom-team careers retain their own drivers while using the selected season's surrounding grid.
- The new season grid is loaded before the pre-season report is displayed.

2. SEASON-BY-SEASON REGULATION PACKAGES
- Added 2016–2026 technical, sporting and financial regulation summaries.
- Includes the 2017 wider-car/aero reset, 2018 Halo introduction, 2019 aerodynamic simplification, 2020 component freeze and COVID calendar changes, 2021 floor/cost-cap changes, 2022 ground-effect reset, 2023 floor/safety refinements, 2024–2025 carry-over rules, and the 2026 active-aero/power-unit reset.
- Major regulation changes now happen at season transitions, not as random mid-season penalties.
- Regulation changes affect the player's car and AI car ratings.
- The season regulations and their in-game department effects are shown in the constructor introduction and pre-season grid briefing.

3. CONTRACT EXPIRY
- Driver contracts are checked at the new-season transition.
- Expired drivers are marked expired, removed from their race seats and released to the driver market.
- Vacancies are filled from available F1 drivers first; an F2 promotion is only used when no suitable F1 driver is available.
- Player-negotiated and extended contracts are tracked separately so historical baseline grids do not erase a valid signing.
- Historical default contract end dates are aligned to each driver's final season in the supplied historical grids, avoiding arbitrary mass expiries that would destroy authentic season line-ups.

4. R&D RATING RECOVERY
- Regulation and mechanical losses are synchronized into the affected individual R&D nodes.
- Upgrading those same nodes can restore the lost department rating.
- Department aggregation now shows a visible point of recovery after an individual component upgrade.
- Part replacement also synchronizes the R&D node values.

TESTING
- Full npm run qa regression suite passed.
- New tests cover 2019 grid migration, all 2016–2026 regulation profiles, one-time regulation resets, AI rating impact, R&D rating recovery, contract expiry and replacement.
- Existing historical transfer, feeder-series, career, archive, market, staff, circuit-map and package checks passed.
- ZIP integrity checked separately after packaging.

LIMITATIONS
- Regulation descriptions summarize the major season-specific technical, sporting and financial changes; they are not a verbatim reproduction of every article in the FIA rulebooks.
- The historical roster data remains season-start grids plus separately implemented headline mid-season swaps; it is not a complete record of every substitute appearance.
- This is a source ZIP. A Windows installer build and full Electron launch were not performed in this environment.
