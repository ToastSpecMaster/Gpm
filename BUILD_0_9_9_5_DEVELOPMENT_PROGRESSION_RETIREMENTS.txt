GRAND PRIX MANAGER 0.9.9.5 — DEVELOPMENT, PROGRESSION & RETIREMENTS

CAR DEVELOPMENT
- Rebuilt the development chooser with separate Aero, Chassis, Suspension, Brakes, Power Unit and Gearbox tabs.
- Aero contains individual front wing, rear wing, floor/underfloor, diffuser, beam wing and bodywork airflow components.
- Chassis, suspension and brake departments each contain individual upgradeable components.
- Each component has its own rating, cost, upgrade action and saved state.
- Power Unit tab displays power and reliability. Works teams can develop their own engine; customer teams are locked and must use supplier contracts.
- Gearbox tab directs supplier-managed teams to their gearbox contract screen.

STAFF
- New and existing careers automatically receive two race engineers, two starting engineers, a strategist and a pit crew if those roles are vacant.
- Starter staff are entry-level and do not consume the hiring budget. They can be replaced through the Staff department.

DRIVER DEVELOPMENT
- Young drivers across player line-ups, AI line-ups, academies and reserve programmes now gain rating gradually as race weekends progress.
- Progress is capped by a stored potential rating and slows as drivers approach age 25; drivers aged 30+ stop receiving automatic growth.
- Academy/reserve development records starts, points and rating progress.

RETIREMENTS
- Career retirement events now store the retirement year and constructor.
- Hall of Fame includes a Driver Retirements table showing driver, team, retirement year, titles and wins.

VERSIONING
- package.json remains at SemVer-compatible 0.9.9 for Electron Builder. The in-game build label is 0.9.9.5.

QA
