GRAND PRIX MANAGER 0.9.9.6 — REALISM / DISPLAY HOTFIX

Based on the uploaded 0.9.9.5 source archive.

Implemented in this patch:
- 3D chassis viewer now retries loading when its initially hidden garage panel becomes visible.
- GLB geometry setup reports an explicit error instead of silently leaving a blank viewer when a model has no visible geometry.
- Car overall rating calculation now validates department values and clamps the computed result to 1–100.
- Technical-state migration initialises missing car departments from the saved car rating and recalculates the overall rating, avoiding stale fallback values.
- AI constructor car ratings now develop between seasons instead of resetting to an unchanging baseline. Growth is bounded and mildly influenced by the player's team reputation.

Not represented as completed in this patch: additional season-specific engine manufacturers, driver portrait assets in the market, original house menu soundtrack, full component failure/repair lifecycle, and championship-title counter presentation. These require dedicated implementation and verification rather than being claimed as finished.

Validation performed:
- JavaScript syntax checks for app.js and car-viewer.js.
- Existing QA suites: circuit maps, pre-1.0, team creator, 0.9.9.2 technical, contract extensions, and 0.9.9.5 development progression.
- Archive integrity checked after packaging.
- Electron GUI visual inspection and Windows installer build were not performed in this environment.
