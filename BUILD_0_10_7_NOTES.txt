GRAND PRIX MANAGER 0.10.7 — TEAM IDENTITY & R&D TREE FIX

FIXES
- Hardened the My Team constructor-name input: explicit focus, visible input state, live value mirroring, and Enter-to-submit support.
- Constructor creation reads the actual typed name and rejects an empty name before creating a save.
- Replaced the flat R&D upgrade-card display with a six-department, three-stage development tree.
- Added explicit prerequisite links, locked/unlocked states, upgrade levels, and persistent development history.
- Added dedicated reliability research nodes for engine durability, gearbox durability, hydraulics, thermal reliability, and electrical reliability.
- Fixed component aggregation: an individual wing/floor/suspension node now contributes proportionally to its department rating instead of replacing the entire department rating.
- Engine development remains restricted to teams operating their own works power unit.
- Purchased R&D levels and latest upgrade messages are saved and survive reopening the chart and season transitions.
- Updated service-worker cache version and included the new runtime module in the Windows Electron package manifest.
